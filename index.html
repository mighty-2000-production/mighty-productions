About the book:

Donald and Maggy are two high-school teenagers who fall deeply in love despite coming from completely different worlds.

Donald is a poor orphan, while Maggy comes from a wealthy family. When their love begins to face challenges they never expected, the two must decide how far they are willing to go to protect what they share.

With family opposition, difficult choices and an uncertain future standing between them, Donald and Maggy find themselves fighting for a love that refuses to surrender.

A story of young love, courage, sacrifice and determination.

Defiant Hearts

By Nosretep

Chapter One

The evening sky was slowly turning dark as the last rays of sunlight disappeared beyond the horizon. Somewhere in the distance, the sound of footsteps echoed through the quiet street.

She stood there silently, holding back the tears that had been gathering in her eyes. Life had never been easy for her, but she had learned one important lesson: sometimes the heart must remain strong even when everything around you seems to be falling apart.

She took a deep breath and looked ahead. She knew that whatever happened next would change everything. There was fear in her heart, but there was also something stronger — determination.

Somewhere beyond the darkness was a new beginning, and she was determined to find it.
